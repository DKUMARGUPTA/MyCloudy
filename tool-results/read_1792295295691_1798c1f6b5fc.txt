   979→ * only the buttons catch clicks. Vertical hit bands are padded to a
   980→ * comfortable minimum (a raw 12pt line is ~16px on screen — too thin), so

   986→  dispW,

   988→  onPick,

   992→  dispH: number;
   993→  onPick: (piece: HarvestPiece) => void;

  1013→              top: `${(top / dispH) * 100}%`,

  1015→              height: `${(bandH / dispH) * 100}%`,

  1750→function arrowHeadPoints(
