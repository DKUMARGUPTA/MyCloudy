DocoPDF — OCR language data (self-hosted, no CDN)

Files in this directory are Tesseract trained models from the
"tessdata_fast" repository, distributed under the Apache License 2.0:
https://github.com/tesseract-ocr/tessdata_fast
(upstream: https://github.com/tesseract-ocr/tess, Apache-2.0)

  eng.traineddata  (~4.0 MB)  English, LSTM "fast" integer model
  hin.traineddata  (~1.1 MB)  Hindi (Devanagari), LSTM "fast" integer model

These are static model files served from this origin only; they never
leave the device after download (browser cache).
